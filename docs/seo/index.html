    The cost of waiting

    What you lose while your site is not set up for search

    A site without SEO is like a shop with no sign down a side street: the goods are good, but people walking past cannot see them.

    01

    Customers who are already searching for you

    Someone typing a service plus your city is ready to buy. If competitors own the results, the enquiry goes to them.

    02

    Money that keeps flowing into ads

    Paid traffic stops when the budget does. A well-configured site keeps bringing people after that.

    03

    Technical problems you cannot see

    Slow loading, duplicate pages and broken markup stop search engines understanding your site, and you never find out.

    04

    Maps and local search

    Without an up-to-date map listing, people nearby will not find you just when they need a service close to them.

    Local search

    Website, SEO, GEO, bot and support work as one system: the site collects enquiries, SEO and GEO bring people in, the bot answers, and support keeps it all alive. When one team does it, you explain your business once and do not stitch results together yourself.
