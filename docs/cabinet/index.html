    Roles and access

    Company owner

    Company projects, inviting colleagues to selected projects, revoking access.

    Colleague

    Assigned projects. Approvals and finance are granted separately.

    Studio manager

    Assigned projects, messages, materials, invoices and payment records.

    Studio owner

    Reliability

    Company data is isolated

    A client only sees the projects, files and invoices assigned to them. Revoked access takes effect immediately.

    Audit trail

    Operations with money or access record who did what, when, and the result.

    Safe sign-in

    Passwords are hashed, confirmation links are single-use and time-limited, sessions can be ended.

    Russian and English

    Interface, emails and errors in both languages, light and dark themes, reduced motion by system setting.

    Portal questions

    Is the portal live?

    This is a preview with demo data for now. The studio first completes two acceptance runs with test clients, then opens registration. Request access and we will let you know.

    Do I need an account to get a quote?

    No. You can send an estimate and request through Telegram without the portal. The portal is for running the project, versions, invoices and support in one place.

    How does a project appear in the portal?

    After the request and contract are agreed, a manager links the project to your account by hand. Until then the Versions and Invoices sections are empty.

    How does version approval work?

    You open a specific version with its repository link and choose Approve or Request changes. Changes need a comment. The decision stays on record with its author and time.
