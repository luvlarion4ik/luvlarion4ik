    Why separate purchases are worse

    01

    Every supplier needs your business explained again

    A designer, developer, SEO specialist and bot builder ask the same questions. You spend weeks retelling and the results still do not fit.

    02

    Nobody owns the whole

    The site is built but not optimised. SEO exists but the bot does not take enquiries. When something fails, each points at the next.

    03

    Decisions are made without each other

    Site structure, search copy and bot flows should be built together. If made separately, they get redone.

    04

    Higher in total

    Each supplier has its own kickoff, set-up and margin. In a bundle the shared work is done once.

    Common questions about bundles

    Can I start small and expand later?

    Yes. Start with “Start” and add SEO, GEO, a bot and support later. We make the first decisions with growth in mind, so nothing needs redoing.

    Does the discount apply to any combination?

    The calculator applies the volume discount when you choose several services. I confirm the exact price after a short call.

    What if I only need one service?

    Then buy one. A bundle is for people who care about the overall result, not a single part.
