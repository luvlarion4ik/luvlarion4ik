    Interier showroom · website and MAX bot

    Kitchen Showroom

    The showroom receives the selection, photo, visualisation and estimate in one enquiry. Staff confirm the final specification and price.

    Brief and build

    Brief
    Help buyers preview a kitchen in their own room, estimate the cost and send the showroom a complete enquiry.
    Build
    We connected the catalogue to a MAX bot that takes a room photo, helps buyers select a kitchen and generates a visualisation and initial estimate. Showroom staff manage the flow in an admin panel.
