From brief to launch

  1. Discovery

    Describe the challenge on Telegram. We review the context and prepare options for our first call.

  2. Project design

    We discuss alternatives together, refine the proposal and agree on the scope in a second meeting.

  3. Agreement

    We document the deliverables, timeline and acceptance criteria. The deposit is agreed individually, usually 50%.

  4. Delivery

    We deliver in stages or in full, address feedback and sign the acceptance documents before final payment.

  5. Support

    We fix issues in the delivered scope for two months at no extra charge. Additional work is estimated separately.

Common questions

How do I price a small project?

Describe one workflow, such as booking a service or enquiring about a catalogue item. We agree on the first release, price and timeline before work starts.

Can we arrange a meeting?

We can start with a video call. For an in-person meeting, send your city and the topics you’d like to cover so we can agree on the format in advance.

Can you deploy on our infrastructure?

Yes. We review your compute capacity, data requirements and access restrictions before selecting the deployment architecture.

Can we work under NDA?

We agree on confidentiality terms before you share restricted materials.

How do revisions and source ownership work?

The agreement sets out the revision scope, source delivery and rights before development begins.

What happens after two months?

Further work is quoted separately. A support subscription is available for ongoing needs.
