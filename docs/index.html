In development

  • Wrist AI

    Preparing for an App Store release

    My AI assistant for Apple Watch. Ask a question and read the answer on the watch.

    Read the case study

    Why I started it

    I kept reaching for my iPhone when I wanted a quick AI answer while wearing my Apple Watch. I hadn’t found an assistant I liked using directly on the watch, so I started building one around a simple flow: ask, read the answer, get back to what you were doing.

    What I built

    The SwiftUI watch app has a question field for typing or dictation, a choice of response modes, and recently asked questions. I also built an iPhone companion with Apple sign-in and a larger screen for chat.

    How it works

    The watch sends the question and the selected mode to a FastAPI backend. I built the server-side routing so short questions can use a quick mode and harder questions can use a reasoning mode. The project includes tests for authentication and model routing.

    What comes next

    I’m preparing the commercial release. I plan to verify the watch and iPhone flow on devices, run a TestFlight beta, and submit Wrist AI to the App Store for review.

  • City Technologies

    B2G / B2C · In development

    A city services platform for the Yamalo-Nenets Autonomous Okrug (YNAO), with a resident-facing MAX Mini App and workspaces for operators and providers.

    Read the case study

    For residents

    The resident-facing app brings the service catalog, request submission and status history together. Delivery scenarios also have a courier bot.

    For operators and service providers

    The service app has a request queue, separate roles for operators and partners, assignment by service type and area, and a view for tracking progress. The B2G side is designed around coordinating requests and the organizations that fulfill them.

    Status

    The development version includes the interfaces and request workflows. Maps and payments are currently simulated.
