AuraWatch

An assistant for iPhone and Apple Watch, currently in closed beta. Silent Prompter displays a suggestion on the dimmed watch face.

Join the closed beta

How Silent Prompter works

  1. Start

    The wearer starts a session on the watch.

  2. Read the suggestion

    Audio is sent to the server for processing and transcription. The response appears on the wrist.

  3. Finish

    Press the Digital Crown three times to stop the session.

Privacy

Session data is stored until the user deletes it or 180 days have passed. Obtain participants’ consent before turning on the microphone.

Which devices are supported?

The first release targets iOS and watchOS. Android and other watch brands are planned.
