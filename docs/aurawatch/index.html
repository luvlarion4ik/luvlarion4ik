    AuraWatch

    AI assistant for Apple Watch and iPhone

    Ask a question from your wrist, get a prompt during a meeting and review the summary on your iPhone.

    Preparing for private beta. Captured in Demo mode.

    See the app ↓Apply for private beta

    The challenge and solution

    The challenge
    Reaching for a phone in the middle of a meeting can break the flow of conversation. The challenge is to put a short answer on the wrist and keep the details available after the meeting.
    The solution
    We’re building two native apps with a shared backend. Silent Prompter shows short prompts on the watch. On iPhone, you can review meeting summaries, continue a chat and add material to your knowledge base.
    1. Ask from the watch
    2. Read the streamed reply
    3. Get prompts during a meeting
    4. Review the summary on iPhone

    The app in action

    Apple Watch and iPhone simulator screens captured in Demo mode.

    The Matrix orb

    On the watch face, 37 dots show what the assistant is doing. Their brightness changes as it listens, processes a question and streams a reply. SwiftUI draws the matrix on the watch.

    How AuraWatch is taking shape

    1. 20 November 2026v0.9

      The first conversation on the wrist

      We connected both apps to a FastAPI backend. The first working build could take a voice question, open a chat and run Silent Prompter.

    2. 20 November 2026v1.0-rc

      A chance to confirm each action

      We added cards on the watch so people could see a proposed action and confirm or cancel it. The app also asks for consent before recording a meeting.

    3. 20 November 2026v1.1-rc

      Context for other assistants

      We built an MCP connector for ChatGPT and Claude. Reading data and making changes each require a separate permission.

    4. 22 November 2026Current stage

      Preparing for private beta

      We’re preparing both apps for private testing. The iPhone build already supports recording and image attachments in chat. In a separate build, we’re trying models that run on the device.
