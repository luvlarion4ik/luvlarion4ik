    Common questions

    Do you work with our 1C configuration?

    Yes. We review it and propose an integration approach.

    What if a field is misread?

    Doubtful fields go to a person. We tune the confidence threshold with you.

    Can we come with just a problem?

    Yes. Describe what is wrong; we will propose a fix and tell you honestly if AI is not needed.

    What does a catalogue price include?

    The “from” price is the minimum scope in the card. The final quote is fixed before we start.

    How can we test an idea first?

    A pilot on one process with agreed metrics; scale only on results.

    What do we get after launch?

    A working system, access, documentation and two months of fixes.

    Can you work with our IT team?

    Yes: your repositories, processes and security rules.

    Where do we start?

    With one example from your work.
