    The cost of waiting

    What you lose while AI does not know you

    People increasingly ask ChatGPT, Alice or Perplexity who to contact. They get one paragraph, not ten links.

    01

    You are missing from the one answer

    In search results you can be tenth. An AI answer usually names a few. If you are not among them, the person never sees you.

    02

    AI builds answers from what it can find

    If your site has no clear services, prices and answers, the model has nothing to say about you and will talk about a competitor instead.

    03

    Competitors are starting earlier

    AI visibility builds over months. Those who start now will appear in answers when your market is only just learning about it.

    04

    You do not know what is being said about you

    Without an audit you cannot tell whether AI names you, names you correctly or what it knows about your company.

    How it works

    Where answers come from

    AI assistants draw on websites, directories and reviews.

    Which information helps

    Clear, structured and consistent details make information about your business easier to find.

    What we improve

    We update your site and listings, then compare AI answers before and after.

    What we do

    1. Audit answers about your business and competitors across 20–30 common questions
    2. Add schema.org markup for your organisation, services, FAQs and prices
    3. Create an llms.txt file
    4. Publish pages that answer common customer questions
    5. Align details across Yandex Business, 2GIS, maps and directories
    6. Repeat the assessment after one to two months
