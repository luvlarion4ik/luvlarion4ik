    What you get

    A map of your business

    Where customers come from, where enquiries leak and what brings people back. Three versions you can move around and extend.

    Numbers in money

    Revenue, growth, what you lose today, build cost and payback for every version.

    A map you keep

    Download the file, copy the link or save it to the portal. Ilia will look at it and send a review.

    How the numbers work

    The estimate rests on your answers and industry averages: average order, the share of enquiries that reach payment, lost enquiries and repeat purchases. Anything you skip is filled with the industry average, and we tell you so. It is a starting point for a conversation; exact numbers come after a review of your business.

    Questions

    Is it really free?

    Yes. You build the map yourself with no payment and no sign-up. The free review from Ilia doesn’t commit you to anything either.

    Where does my data go?

    Nowhere. Answers and maps are stored in your browser only. They reach us when you send the map via Telegram yourself.

    How accurate are the numbers?

    They are an estimate from your answers and industry averages. The more detail you add, the closer to real life. We do an exact calculation after a review.

    What happens once the map is ready?

    Hit “Get a free review”. Ilia will look at the map and send a review with price and timeline. The decision is yours.

    Want Ilia to look at your map?

    Send it on Telegram: you’ll know what to build first and what it costs.
