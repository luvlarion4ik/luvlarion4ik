    Why not an external AI service

    01

    Customer data leaves your perimeter

    A request to an external AI service passes text, documents and personal data to a third party, often abroad.

    02

    No proof of where processing happened

    Security teams ask not “where is the server” but “how can you prove it”. Usually there is no answer.

    03

    One leak costs more than the project

    Fines for personal-data leaks have grown sharply; a repeat leak is a share of revenue.

    04

    Models may learn from your data

    Public services make it hard to guarantee that requests never feed someone else’s model.

    Risk

    What a single leak costs

    Fines under Article 13.11 of the Russian Code of Administrative Offences for companies since 30 May 2025. These are amounts per violation, before customer claims, inspections and reputational damage.

    ViolationFine for a company
    Leak of data on 1,000–10,000 peopleRUB 3–5 million
    Leak of data on 10,000–100,000 peopleRUB 5–10 million
    Leak of data on more than 100,000 peopleRUB 10–15 million
    Leak of special categories (e.g. health data)up to RUB 15 million
    Leak of biometric dataup to RUB 20 million
    Failure to notify Roskomnadzor of a leakRUB 1–3 million
    Repeat leak1–3% of revenue, RUB 20–500 million

    A private AI perimeter costs less than one such fine and does not require sending customer data to external services.

    Source: Article 13.11 of the Russian Code of Administrative Offences as amended by Federal Law No. 420-FZ. For reference only, not legal advice.

    1. 1HostingA contract with a Russian data centre, site details and hardware identifiers — the kind of documents listed in Roskomnadzor’s guidance on proving localisation.
    2. 2Technical controlsNo external LLM APIs, restricted outbound traffic, databases, monitoring and backups inside the perimeter.
    3. 3Request logFor each request: ID, time, model and compute node. Records are tamper-evident and can be exported.

    Common questions

    Is this a 152-FZ compliance certificate?

    No. We evidence facts: where the infrastructure is and how requests were processed. Whether your processes comply with the law is assessed separately.

    Can you deploy on our premises without internet?

    Yes. We size the model for your hardware and deploy it in your environment. Hardware is quoted separately.

    Which models do you use?

    Open models that run locally, such as the Qwen family. The choice depends on the task, language and hardware.
